Your health data belongs at home.

Why I'm building this

I'm Philipp, an IT consultant from Germany. I've spent two decades running infrastructure for companies — and the last few years watching consumer tech move the other way: everything becomes a cloud, everything becomes a subscription, and now our most private data is being fed into AI systems nobody can look inside.

Health data is the data you can't change. You can replace a leaked credit card; you can't replace your heart. Nobody knows what will be done with these records in ten years — so the safest place for them is your own home.

People who work in IT can do more than complain. We can build the alternative and prove it's better. wristpull is my attempt: local-first, open source, and soon simple enough for people who never want to touch a terminal.

Your data stays with you

Every file your devices write is archived untouched, byte for byte. One SQLite database holds every heartbeat since 2015 — readable by any tool, forever.

Your Garmin export is no longer a dead end: wristpull imports all ten years of it.

How your data is kept

You see what leaves

The AI coach gets a few hundred words of daily aggregates — never raw data, GPS or serial numbers. Every statistic is computed on your machine first; the model only narrates. Local model or cloud model: your choice, per run.

A dry run writes the exact prompt to disk before anything is sent.

How the coach works

A complete system

Watch over USB, band over Bluetooth without a phone, a dashboard for your phone that does everything the terminal does, a readiness index you can take apart, correlations with sweet spots, a coach — and your training plan pushed back to the watch.

Not a script. A system you live with — no terminal required.
